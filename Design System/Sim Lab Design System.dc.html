Sim Lab / Design system
v1.0 · achromatic instrument
Foundations for Sim Lab

Brightness is the ranking. Colour belongs to the cards.

Sim Lab is an instrument, not a dashboard. Its interface is entirely greyscale — hierarchy, state and outcome are carried by brightness, weight and position. The only chroma in the product is the five Magic colour-identity pips, so any colour a user sees means one thing: this belongs to the game.

Every value here is drawn from the production build, corrected for WCAG 2.2 AA. Contrast ratios are computed against #0A0A0A.

01The five laws
Brightness ranks

The winner is the brightest thing in the row. Never a badge, never a hue.

Colour is domain-only

W U B R G pips and nothing else. No coloured buttons, links or statuses.

Hairlines, not cards

Content sits on the page. Regions are separated by 1px rules, never containers.

Left-aligned, wide measure

Prose caps at 76ch. Nothing centres except a genuinely empty state.

Fixes subtract

If a problem needs a new decorated component, it is the wrong solution.

07Buttons and links
Set up a run Validate only Focused Import a deck Hovered link

Bone #E8E4DA is the only fill. One primary per view — the action that costs the most time. Everything else is #161616 with a border, or a bone link with a rule under it.

Focus is 2px #FFFFFF at 2px offset on every interactive element — maximum contrast on this page, and it keeps the achromatic rule intact. Never disable a primary button. Keep it live and explain the blocker beside it.

The identifying attribute sits immediately after the control — never a wide empty gutter before the name. Machine strings are right of it as metadata. Whole row is the link, so no repeated “Watch” column.

Win-rate bars are 2px with a 1px tick at 25% — the even-seats baseline for a four-player pod. The leading deck's bar and figure go to #FFFFFF; everything else is #8A8A8A.

13Accessibility contractWCAG 2.2 AA
1.4.3All text at or above 4.6:1. ink-4 is the floor; nothing dimmer carries words.
1.4.1No meaning carried by hue alone. Winner is position plus brightness; status is dot shape.
2.4.7 · 2.4.132px white ring at 2px offset on every focusable element. The replay is keyboard-first — it needs this most.
2.5.856px row targets, 42–48px controls. Comfortably past the 24px floor and the 44px platform guidance.
3.3.2Every field has a persistent visible label. Placeholder is a format hint only.
4.1.3Run completion announces via a live region and swaps the header in place. The page never grows a second heading underneath itself.
