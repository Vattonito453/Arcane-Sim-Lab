The fill carries its own scrim. --winrate-fill starts at deep teal rgba(13,58,68,.95) and only reaches bright cyan past 46%, so the label zone is always dark ground. Row height 64px, gap 8px, radius 8px. Thumbnail is flush to the left edge with matching corner radius — no inset, no gap.

The fill sits behind the text, width = win %, radius 6px — chart and row background at once, so the panel needs no axis. Its gradient is dark for a fixed 165px — absolute stops, not percentages — so the label zone stays legible at every win rate.

13Background imageryart direction & the three-layer stack
Art direction

High-fantasy landscape on the plane of Dominaria: ancient moss-covered stone archways and ruins, floating diamond hedron monoliths drifting through an atmospheric sky, subtle glowing blue-violet mana leylines in the clouds. Palette runs cool slate blue, deep emerald moss, warm cyan and violet horizon glow. Twilight, epic and still — the art is a stage, never a subject; nothing in it should compete with the mascot or the data.

The three-layer stack
1Art — full-bleed, object-fit:cover, anchored top.
2Linear scrim — --backdrop-scrim, light at the sky so the hedrons read, heavy at the base so the data panel wins.
3Radial vignette — --backdrop-vignette, rgba(13,15,18,.60) at the edges, framing the UI toward centre.

Beneath all three sits --atmosphere — a twilight sky gradient with two leyline glows. It carries the masthead on its own if the art fails to load, so the page never renders as a black rectangle.

§3.2's Unsplash URL is superseded

The source spec hardcodes an external photo into body. Three reasons it does not ship: the photo is woodland, not Dominaria ruins; an external hotlink breaks offline and can rot; and Unsplash requires visible attribution the rule provides no place for. The local plate has none of those problems. background-attachment: fixed is also dropped — it is unreliable on iOS and costly to repaint; use .backdrop--app, which is position-fixed and achieves the same lock.
